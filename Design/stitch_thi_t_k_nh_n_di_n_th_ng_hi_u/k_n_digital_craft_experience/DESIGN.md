## Brand & Style

This design system embodies **Modern Heritage / Contemporary Vietnamese Craft**—a sophisticated dialogue between the centuries-old fan-making mastery of Chàng Sơn craft village and contemporary digital editorial design. 

### Brand Personality & Audience
- **Poetic & Tactile:** Evoking the fibrous texture of traditional Dó paper, the organic warmth of hand-bent bamboo ribs, and the refined luster of natural lacquer and mulberry silk.
- **Cultivated & Intellectual:** Designed for culture enthusiasts, contemporary artisans, digital archivists, and discerning users seeking authentic cultural resonance within high-fidelity digital platforms.
- **Restrained Luxury:** Prioritizes breathing room, high typographic contrast, and intentional ornamental moments (such as crimson seal marks and stylized fan rib grids) over ornamental clutter.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Form
The system adopts an editorial **Minimalist Tactile** framework. Structural foundations rely on clean architectural layouts, deliberate negative space, and disciplined typography, enriched with subtle organic textures:
- **Dó & Điệp Paper Depth:** Soft, warm-white surfaces replacing clinical digital white.
- **Lacquer & Metallic Accents:** Heritage lacquer crimson anchors primary interactive states, balanced by hand-planed bamboo tones and silk gold highlights.
- **Micro-Motifs:** Vectorized lacquered seals (*con dấu triện*), delicate structural fan spines (*nan tre*), and stylized motifs of Chim Lạc and sacred lotus silhouettes applied strictly as ambient structural anchors or watermarks.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the artisan materials of Chàng Sơn: natural mineral pigments, cured lacquer sap, split bamboo culms, and handmade mulberry paper.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Brand (`#8F2924` - Sơn Mài Red):** Used for focal points, high-priority interactive triggers, focal badges, and official stamp indicators.
- **Deep Accent (`#6F1D1B` - Deep Crimson Sap):** Active/pressed states for primary elements, borders on high-emphasis containers, and deep editorial headlines.
- **Secondary (`#C99A52` - Bamboo Gold):** Structural lines, subtle dividers evoking fan ribs, iconography strokes, and curated highlight tags.
- **Silk Gold (`#E7BD76`):** Secondary highlights, delicate hover halos, and decorative badge fills.
- **Neutral Primary (`#302720` - Mực Tàu Ink):** Deep charred-wood tone for all primary body text, titles, and dense data, avoiding harsh synthetic black (`#000000`).
- **Neutral Secondary (`#76685C` - Weathered Wood):** Captions, secondary descriptors, inactive icons, and metadata.
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:**
  - Primary Canvas: `#FDF8F0` (Giấy Điệp / Warm Ivory).
  - Secondary Canvas: `#F5EBDD` (Mộc Dó Paper / Sanded Bamboo).
  - Surface Containers: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Mulberry Silk).
- **System States:**
  - Success: `#66866B` (Patinated Celadon).
  - Disabled / Outline: `#D9D0C3` (Raw Bamboo Fiber).

### Usage Rules
- Primary Canvas (`#FDF8F0`) serves as the foundational backdrop; `#FFFFFF` is reserved strictly for elevated foreground cards and interactive surfaces to preserve tactile warmth.
- Crimson Seal marks must always utilize `#8F2924` with high visual prominence, acting as marks of authenticity.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between classical Vietnamese literary tradition and modern digital legibility.

### Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the cultural gravitas of printed anthologies, woodblock carvings, and literary journals. It features high stroke contrast, elegant terminals, and pronounced serifs suited for heroic editorial headers, product titles, and storytelling anchors.
- **Body & UI Labels (`Be Vietnam Pro`):** Purpose-designed for Vietnamese diacritics. It guarantees optimal spacing and baseline harmony across complex tone marks (`dấu hỏi`, `dấu ngã`, `dấu nặng`), ensuring functional clarity in dense cultural commentary and craft instruction.

### Typographic Guidelines
- All uppercase display headings must include a subtle positive tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) to emulate traditional stone and wood engraving.
- Vietnamese diacritical marks must never be visually clipped by tight line heights; `Be Vietnam Pro` line heights are maintained at a minimum of `1.5x` body size for clear vertical clearance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a **desktop-first editorial grid** calibrated to 1440px viewport widths with a centralized 1280px maximum content container.

### Grid & Compositional Rhythm
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column symmetrical grid. Max container width: 1280px centered. Column gutters: `2rem` (32px), outer canvas margin: `5rem` (80px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column fluid grid. Column gutters: `1.5rem` (24px), outer margins: `2.5rem` (40px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column responsive grid. Gutters: `1rem` (16px), outer margins: `1.25rem` (20px).

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing reflects the balanced symmetry of traditional fan blades spreading outward from a central pivot:
- **Asymmetrical Editorial Breathing Room:** Sections alternate between wide structural negative spaces (`space-xl` * 2) and compact inspection panels (`space-md`) for artifact details.
- **Structural Nan Tre Rhythm:** Vertical dividers throughout multi-column cards mimic the slender profile of fan ribs, spaced with uniform horizontal rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical, tactile paper-layering principles rather than heavy synthetic drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **Tonal Surface Tiers**, **Bamboo-Tension Hairlines**, and **Organic Warm Ambient Glows**.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FDF8F0` (Giấy Điệp). Flat, non-elevated foundation.
- **Tier 1 (Layered Dó Paper / Inset Panels):** `#F5EBDD`. Used to carve out secondary workshop sections, step-by-step carving workflows, or meta sidebars.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Silk Sheet / Primary Cards):** `#FFFFFF`. Floats subtly above the canvas with a low-opacity warm ambient shadow (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(48, 39, 32, 0.05)`).
- **Tier 3 (Active Dialogs & Craft Modals):** `#FFFFFF` paired with a double-layered shadow tinted with warm lacquer undertones: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(48, 39, 32, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(143, 41, 36, 0.04)`.

### Border & Hairline Framework
Instead of high-contrast elevation breaks, surfaces are delineated by refined `1px` hairlines:
- Border default: `#D9D0C3` (Raw Bamboo Fiber).
- Border interactive/active: `#C99A52` (Bamboo Gold) or `#8F2924` (Sơn Mài Red).
- Stylized filigree corners or subtle inset lines reflect the structural bindings of Chàng Sơn fan joints.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is set to **Level 1 (Soft)**. This preserves the crisp, disciplined architecture of traditional wood joinery and parchment trimming, avoiding overly bubbly or playful aesthetics.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Tags):** `0.25rem` (4px). Provides a clean, hand-trimmed paper edge.
- **Container Surfaces (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px). Used for product exhibition cards, modal dialogs, and craft preview viewports.
- **Hero Containers (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px). Reserved for primary display media frames or hero craft-studio canvases.
- **Con Dấu Triện (Seal Badges):** Strictly square with softened 2px corners or sharp circular stamps, mimicking hand-carved stone chops.
- **Fan Arcs:** Where curved geometry is structurally necessary (such as fan silhouette preview windows or motif viewports), continuous parabolic arcs (G2 continuity) are used to reflect hand-bent bamboo spines.
